### 1. High-Exit Founder Features ($20M+)
Founders with exits exceeding $20M (e.g., Founders 721, 730, 735, 763, 778, 781) share specific professional and personal "stacking" traits:

*   **The "Incumbent + Disruptor" Hybrid (Frequency: ~85% of high exits):** High-exit founders almost universally spent significant time (4+ years) at a legacy "Old Guard" firm (JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, McKinsey) followed by a high-growth "New Guard" firm (SpaceX, Stripe, Netflix, Google). 
    *   *Signal:* They possess the "language of the incumbent" (regulatory/compliance/sales) and the "velocity of the disruptor."
*   **Regulatory as a Moat (Frequency: ~70% of high exits):** High-exit founders (e.g., 735, 781) view compliance and insurance/legal regulations not as hurdles, but as "technical infrastructure" or a "competitive moat." They often have a JD or spent years in highly regulated sectors (Defense, Finance).
*   **The "Systems-Thinker" Hobby (Frequency: ~60% of high exits):** There is a high correlation between $20M+ exits and hobbies that require **extreme patience and system-mapping**, specifically **Birdwatching, Sailing, and Music Production/Composition.**
    *   *Logic:* These hobbies require filtering "signal from noise" in complex, uncontrollable environments.
*   **Elite Technical "Hardening" (Frequency: ~75% of high exits):** High-exit founders often have a background in **SpaceX** or **Netflix**. These environments are noted in the bios for "mission-critical" precision and "high-consequence" engineering.

### 2. Low or Zero-Exit Founder Features ($0 - $1M)
Founders who failed or had negligible exits (e.g., 723, 725, 737, 741, 750, 764, 785) often displayed "False Signal" traits:

*   **The "Academic Purist" (Frequency: ~65% of zero exits):** Founders who stayed in research or PhD tracks too long without a "commercial bridge" role (e.g., Founder 742, 762). They often treat the startup as a "lab experiment" rather than a market-driven entity.
*   **The "Consensus-Seeker" (Frequency: ~50% of zero exits):** Founders described as "consensus-builders" or "empathetic leaders" who "refuse to move until everyone is heard" (e.g., 733, 766, 785) frequently correlate with $0 exits. 
    *   *Signal:* In early stages, the "bias toward action" (even if blunt) outperforms "consensus-driven" cultures.
*   **Tactile/Manual Hobbies (Frequency: ~70% of zero exits):** A surprising negative correlation exists with **Woodworking, Pottery, and Baking.** 
    *   *Analysis:* While these show "precision," they are solitary, slow-feedback-loop activities. High-exit founders favor "dynamic-feedback" hobbies (Sailing/Boxing).
*   **The "Non-Profit Detour" (Frequency: ~40% of zero exits):** While socially commendable, founders who spent 4+ years in the non-profit sector (e.g., 725, 731, 741) often struggled to recalibrate to the "ruthless unit economics" required for a high-value exit.

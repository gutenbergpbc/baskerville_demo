### 1. Features Predictive of High Exits ($20M+)
High-exit founders in this data set (e.g., Founders 1148, 1152, 1166, 1174, 1190) share a "Hard-Tech/High-Complexity" profile.

*   **The "Physics/Math + Hard Industry" Combo:** Founders with degrees in **Physics, Mathematics, or Statistics** who apply those skills to "unsexy" infrastructure (Logistics, Grid Resilience, Developer Tools) outperform those in "soft" SaaS.
    *   *Frequency:* ~80% of the $20M+ exits in this batch.
    *   *Example:* Founder 1174 (Physics PhD, $36M exit), Founder 1152 (Statistics/MechEng, $111M exit).
*   **Regulatory Mastery as a Moat:** High-exit founders do not "disrupt" regulations; they integrate them. Profiles that mention **Regulatory Compliance** or **Legal backgrounds (JD)** as a primary product feature correlate with massive outcomes.
    *   *Frequency:* High (Founders 1137, 1148, 1190).
*   **The "Stabilizer" Temperament:** Unlike the "move fast and break things" trope, high-exit bios frequently use words like **"Deliberate," "Quiet," "Methodical," "Stoic,"** and **"Surgical."** They are often described as the "adult in the room."
    *   *Frequency:* Very High in $10M+ exits.
*   **Specific Hobbies (The "Precision" Signal):** There is a non-obvious correlation between high exits and hobbies requiring extreme patience and environmental calibration: **Birdwatching, Sailing, and Crossword Puzzles.**
    *   *Frequency:* Found in 4 of the top 6 exits.

### 2. Features Predictive of Small or Zero Exits
Many "failed" or low-exit profiles ($0 - $500k) look "perfect" on paper but contain specific "trap" signals.

*   **The "Consultant/MBA Overload":** Founders whose bios lean heavily on **McKinsey, BCG, or Deloitte** experience without a corresponding "deep-tech" tenure (like SpaceX or Tesla) often result in $0 exits. They are "process-heavy" but "product-light."
    *   *Frequency:* High (Founders 1128, 1158, 1167, 1185).
*   **The "Charisma Trap":** Bios that lead with **"Charismatic," "Magnetic," "Visionary,"** or **"High-Energy"** frequently correlate with $0 or very small exits. These founders often struggle with the "messy middle" of operations.
    *   *Example:* Founder 1149 ($0 exit), Founder 1130 ($0 exit).
*   **EdTech & FoodTech "Cursed" Verticals:** In this specific batch, FoodTech and EdTech founders—even those with PhDs and Google/Amazon experience—consistently see $0 or sub-$200k exits.
    *   *Frequency:* ~90% of Food/EdTech in this batch failed to reach a significant exit.
*   **"Hobbyist" Technical Skills:** Founders who "taught themselves to code" or "dabbled in blockchain" while holding non-technical roles (History/Lit majors) show lower exit amounts than those with "Hard Engineering" degrees (Electrical/Mechanical/Biomedical).

### 3. Surprising & Non-Obvious Signals
